The manual

Everything the mine does, and how to check it.

The coin, the hourly hSOL drop, every grade, upgrade, quest, button and command, the API behind the numbers, and how to verify any of it yourself.

01

What M1NT is

$M1NT is a Solana coin launched on pump.fun. Every buy and sell pays a creator fee. Once an hour the fees are claimed, swapped into hSOL (Helius staked SOL) and paid to the people holding the coin as Privacy Rings confidential transfers: anyone can see that a wallet was paid, only that wallet can see how much.

The mine is a game on top. Your browser does real SHA-256 proof of work, and rarer hashes give rarer finds. Finds earn XP, upgrades, quests and a place on the miners board. They are not tokens and they are not paid in hSOL; holding $M1NT is what earns the drop.

Before launch no contract address exists. The drop panels say that the engine starts at launch instead of showing numbers. Anyone posting a contract address before it appears on this site is not us.

02

Quick start

  1. Open the mine. m1nt/mine works in any modern browser, on a phone too.
  2. Click a rock or press M. Your miner walks over and starts swinging; each swing is a batch of hashes. Walk with W A S D and drag to look round the cavern.
  3. Read the log. Dust, ore, gems, crystals and cores land in your pack with an XP drop.
  4. Bank with B when the 28 slots are full, walk to the chest and press E, or turn on auto-bank.
  5. Upgrade at the smithy (F): a bigger pickaxe and more hands mean more hashes a swing.
  6. Get on the board: pick a name on the Board tab and press U. The server re-hashes every find before it counts.
  7. Connect a wallet (top right) to see your $M1NT, your share, your estimated next drop and whether your private wallet is set up.

03

How mining works

Every hash is SHA-256 of one line of text:

m1nt|<rock>|<seed>|<nonce>
  • rock is 0 to 9 (rock 1 on screen is 0).
  • seed is your board name in lowercase plus six random characters, like anon-s32zd0. A new name makes a new seed.
  • nonce is a counter that only goes up. It is saved before every swing, so you never hash the same line twice.

The result is 64 hex characters. What counts is how many zero bits it starts with: each leading 0 digit is 4 bits, then the first other digit adds 3 (for 1), 2 (2–3), 1 (4–7) or 0 (8–f). Every extra bit is twice as rare.

A swing runs your pickaxe’s batch of hashes on every hand at once, each hand in its own Web Worker. The best hash of the swing decides what you find; a swing takes at least 1.4 seconds.

Chances are for the best hash of a 250,000-hash swing reaching that grade or better. Type odds in the terminal for your own numbers.

Rocks, veins and the pet

  • Each rock gives 3 to 6 finds, then stays empty for 15 to 32 seconds. With auto on, you walk to the next rock by yourself.
  • Every 100 to 200 seconds (the first after 45 to 90) one rock becomes a rich vein for 45 seconds: finds there go into your pack twice. XP is not doubled, so the board stays honest.
  • If a find’s hash ends in 00 (1 find in 256) and you have no pet yet, a rock golem starts following you. It is only for looks, and anyone can check the roll from the hash.

06

The live mine

The mine is shared. Every open terminal sends a small heartbeat every 15 seconds: your board name (or anon), the rock you work, your level, pickaxe, hands and hash rate. Nothing else.

  • Other miners walk to the rocks they work, in the shirt they picked (or one picked from their name), with their name and level over their heads. They drop off 45 seconds after they leave.
  • Verified finds: when you find a gem, a crystal or a core, it is sent with the next heartbeat. The server re-hashes it, and only then does it show in everyone’s feed and ticker.
  • Gold Rush: every gem or better that anyone finds fills a shared meter. At 40 in ten minutes the rush starts, and every find pays double loot for everybody until the pace drops. XP stays the same, so the board stays honest.
  • Emotes: gm, gn, gg, lfg, wagmi, hodl, nice, rich, ty, o7. Type one, or press it on the Live tab, and a speech bubble shows over your miner for 20 seconds. There is no free chat, so nobody can post links or fake contract addresses.
  • The home page shows how many miners are digging right now.

13

The log

  • Messages are sorted into channels: Game (mining, finds, quests, smithy), Chain (prices, hSOL, slots, the drop), Wallet (wallet and swaps) and Board. All shows everything.
  • Expand (or T) makes the chat box tall; Shrink puts it back. The setting is remembered.
  • Colours: yellow is an answer, cyan a link or a name, teal a match or a gem, purple a crystal, gold a quest or a core, red something that failed.

14

Wallet and swaps

  • The terminal uses the Wallet Standard, so Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and any compatible wallet show up by themselves. On a phone, Phantom and Solflare open the mine in their own browser.
  • Connecting only shares your public address. The terminal reads your SOL, hSOL and $M1NT through /api/wallet and works out your share of supply and your estimated next drop.
  • Swaps go through Jupiter: SOL into hSOL, or SOL into $M1NT after launch. You get a quote first; the swap is built by Jupiter and sent to your wallet, which shows it to you, signs it and sends it. Slippage is 0.5%.
  • M1NT never holds your funds and never asks for a seed phrase or a private key.

15

The hourly drop

At the top of every UTC hour, once the coin and the engine are live:

MinuteStepWhat happens
:00ClaimThe engine claims the creator fees from both pump.fun vaults, the bonding curve’s and PumpSwap’s.
:01StakeThe SOL is swapped into hSOL, so the pot keeps earning staking yield.
:02ShieldThe hSOL moves into the Privacy Rings default ring.
:03SendConfidential transfers go to every holder with a private wallet, sized by how much $M1NT they hold.
  • Pools, bonding curves and exchange vaults are program accounts. They are skipped, and so are holders without a private wallet; their share goes to the holders who can receive it.
  • The Drop tab reads the next split straight from the chain: the vault balances, the hSOL that buys, and each holder’s part. The ledger lists every engine transaction.
Status: the engine starts at launch. Until it runs, the site says so instead of showing numbers.

16

The engine

The engine is one serverless function, /api/engine, that Vercel’s scheduler calls at the top of every hour with a secret only the scheduler has. Anyone else who calls it gets its public status instead.

StepWhat it does
ClaimWhen the two pump.fun creator vaults hold 0.02 SOL or more, it claims them with the official pump.fun SDK.
StakeEverything above a 0.03 SOL reserve for fees is swapped into hSOL through Jupiter.
ShieldAll the hSOL is deposited into the engine’s own Privacy Rings balance.
Find holdersIt reads every $M1NT holder from the chain, skips program accounts (pools, curves, vaults), and asks the Privacy Rings registry which of the largest 2,000 have a private wallet.
SendUp to 225 holders with a private wallet, largest first, are paid in proportion to what they hold, 15 to a transaction. A share under 0.00001 hSOL stays in the pot for the next hour.
  • Dry run first. Until it is switched to live, every run only reads and simulates: it measures the vaults, quotes the swap, checks the prover and counts who would be paid, and moves nothing.
  • Once an hour, at most. A live run takes a lock for its hour, so a retry or a second call in the same hour does nothing.
  • Its key never leaves the server. The creator wallet’s key is a secret in the hosting settings, never in the code; the engine refuses to run if that key is not the creator and engine address the site shows.
  • Proofs: each encrypted transfer carries a zero-knowledge proof from Helius’s attested prover, so the chain can check it without seeing the amount. Who was paid stays public; how much does not.
  • Public status: /api/engine lists the last 24 hours of runs with what was claimed, swapped, shielded and sent, to how many wallets, and the transactions. /api/engine?addr=… says whether one address has a private wallet. The Drop and Wallet tabs show both.

17

Privacy Rings

  • Solana Privacy Rings are built by Helius and live on mainnet since November 2026. In the default ring the asset and the amount are encrypted; the sender and the receiver stay public.
  • An encrypted transfer can only go to a private wallet: a set of keys published for your address. Set one up once and every drop after that reaches you.
  • Helius lists audits by Certora, OtterSec and ZK Security. Read more at helius.dev/privacy.

18

Verify it yourself

Any find can be checked without trusting this site. In a browser console:

const line = 'm1nt|2|anon-s32zd0|759772318';
const buf = await crypto.subtle.digest('SHA-256', new TextEncoder().encode(line));
[...new Uint8Array(buf)].map(b => b.toString(16).padStart(2, '0')).join('');
// '00000d9b5f80dbbb6c7977a5e9d0bc970aa0604d7b5226cd4473807fee0dc3c8'

In a terminal:

printf 'm1nt|2|anon-s32zd0|759772318' | sha256sum

Five leading zeros are 20 bits, and d (1101) adds none: 20 zero bits, hSOL ore. The server does the same sum at /api/verify?rock=2&seed=anon-s32zd0&nonce=759772318, and the export command downloads all your finds in this form.

20

Where the numbers come from

  • Solana RPC: the hSOL stake pool account (its total lamports and pool token supply give the rate), epoch info, performance samples for TPS, and token balances.
  • Jupiter: SOL and hSOL prices, quotes and swaps.
  • Sanctum: the hSOL APY; when the latest epoch reads zero, the average since launch.
  • Dexscreener: $M1NT price, market cap, liquidity, volume and the chart, after launch.
  • pump.fun: the creator-fee vaults of the bonding curve and PumpSwap.
  • Neon Postgres: the miners board.

When a source is down the site shows a dash, never a guess.

21

Privacy and safety

  • In your browser (local storage): your progress, finds, upgrades, quests, settings, name and the last wallet you used. Reset wipes it.
  • On the server: what you submit to the board (name, rock, seed, nonce, hash, bits, XP and time); while a terminal is open, its heartbeat (a random id, name, rock, level, pickaxe, hands, hash rate, emote and shirt colour), which is deleted after an hour; your gem-or-better finds for the feed, kept seven days; and a per-address counter that limits requests.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. The terminal never asks for a seed phrase or a private key; wallets sign in their own window.
  • The only contract address is the one this site shows after launch.

22

Risks

  • No trading means no fees, and no fees means no drops.
  • hSOL moves with the price of SOL.
  • Privacy Rings is new software.
  • The engine is not running until launch, and it depends on pump.fun, Jupiter, Helius and the Privacy Rings prover being up; an hour they are not, nothing is paid and the fees wait for the next run.
  • This is a memecoin. Only put in what you can afford to lose. Nothing here is financial advice.
