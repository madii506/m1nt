    06 — Questions

    Asked at the mine shaft.

    What is M1NT?

    A coin with a mine attached. Its creator fees are turned into hSOL and paid to the people holding it, every hour, as encrypted transfers.

    What is hSOL?

    Helius Staked SOL: SOL staked with Helius's validator, held as a token. It is worth a little more SOL every epoch, so a payout keeps growing while you hold it. Right now 1 hSOL = — SOL.

    How often are payouts?

    Every hour, at the top of the UTC hour, once the engine is running. Each drop is split by how much $M1NT each wallet holds at that moment.

    Who can see what I was paid?

    Everyone can see that the mine paid your wallet. Only you can decrypt the asset and the amount.

    Do I need to do anything?

    Two things, once. Hold $M1NT in a wallet you control, and set up a Privacy Rings private wallet for that address: an encrypted transfer can only go to a private wallet, not a plain address. Holders without one, and program accounts like pools, curves and exchange vaults, are skipped that hour and their share goes to the holders who can receive it.

    Is the mining game real?

    Yes. Your browser computes real SHA-256 proofs of work, and anyone can re-check any find. It earns XP and a place on the miners board, not tokens. Holding $M1NT is what earns hSOL.

    Is the token live?

    Not yet. When it is, the contract address appears right here. Anyone posting one before that is not us.

    What can go wrong?

    No trading means no fees and no drops. hSOL moves with SOL's price. Privacy Rings is new software. This is a memecoin: only put in what you can lose.
